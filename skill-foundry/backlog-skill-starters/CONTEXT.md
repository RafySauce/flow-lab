# Backlog — Skill Starters (INBOX)

Incoming work for the skill-foundry:

- **`sp-<slug>.md`** — skill-primer-briefs (`truth-level: to-review`), from a person or from a flow-foundry Layer-3 gap.
- **Foreign starters** — external material at `truth-level: claimed`, vetted (`../templates/intake-vetting-checklist.md`) before anything is built.

Starters leave here built and staged in `../review-skills/` for the human gate. When the operator promotes the built skill from `../review-skills/` to `../../produced-skills/`, its primer brief leaves the backlog at the same moment for `../completed-skill-starters/` (the intake record's terminal home, `truth-level: verified`). A starter can also be dropped with a decision-log entry — in that case the brief stays here, stamped `status: dead`, per the retention rule. Only `data-class: public` starters belong in this public repo.

The backlog is seeded with the foundry-support skills the foundries' own workflow steps demand — see the `sp-*.md` files here.
