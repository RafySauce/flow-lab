# intake/ — Context

## What this room is for

The unsorted landing zone. Raw thoughts, chat snippets, links, half-formed ideas, and anything that doesn't yet have a home land here. Nothing is refused entry. Nothing stays here forever.

## Process

1. **Capture without friction.** Drop it in. No frontmatter required at capture time. A filename and a date are enough.
2. **Route periodically.** The lightweight tally task reads this room and flags clusters. When a cluster is named, Cincinnatus routes items to the right project room or lets them sit until the heavyweight task proposes a new room.
3. **Promote if signal-bearing.** If an intake item turns out to contain a decision, a design insight, or a capability pattern worth keeping, promote it: move the substance to `corpus/` as a work product, and if the interaction itself was notable, create a trace in `traces/`.
4. **Retire routed items.** Once an intake item has been routed or promoted, it can be archived or deleted. The room should trend toward empty, not toward a growing pile.

## What lives here

- Raw captures: notes, chat excerpts, links, voice-to-text drops
- Unsorted thoughts that belong to a project but haven't been routed yet
- Incoming items from any life domain (homelab, hermes, household, community — whatever arrives)

## What does NOT live here

- Finished work products (→ `corpus/`)
- Promoted usage traces (→ `traces/`)
- Anything already claimed by a project room

## What good work looks like

The room is processed, not hoarded. Items move. The lightweight tally has something to read, but the pile doesn't grow unboundedly. When Cincinnatus drops something here, he trusts it will surface at the right moment — either via the tally flagging a cluster or via a routing pass.

## Naming

Intake files: `YYYY-MM-DD-[short-description].md` — no prefix required, no DNA frontmatter required at capture time.
