## ⚠️ Critical Allergy Alert — Read First

**F1 has a severe peanut and tree nut allergy (EpiPen prescribed).**

This affects every part of trip planning:
- **Flights:** Inform flight attendant at boarding. Request nut-free zone if possible. Avoid economy snack packs with nuts.
- **Restaurants:** Flag allergy at reservation and again at table — every meal.
- **International:** Research local cuisine for hidden nut ingredients (e.g., Thai, Indonesian, North African, Middle Eastern dishes often contain nuts). Know how to say "I am allergic to peanuts and tree nuts — this is a medical emergency" in the destination language. Have a translation card printed.
- **Hotels:** Flag to concierge if ordering in-room dining.
- **Markets / street food:** Extra caution; cross-contamination risk is high.
- **EpiPen location:** Must be on F1's person or immediately accessible at all times — never in checked luggage, never buried in a bag.
