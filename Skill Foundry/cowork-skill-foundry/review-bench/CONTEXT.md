# Review Bench

## What this room is for
The human gate. A normalized skill sits at truth-level: to-review until Cincinnatus reviews it here. This room exists to keep verified meaningful — nothing graduates to completed-skills/ without a human pass. It is deliberately separate from /forge so "built" and "approved" never blur.

## Process
1. The foundry presents the normalized skill (both artifacts) with a short summary: what it does, why it triggers, what it is not, its execution-tier and the reasoning.
2. Cincinnatus reviews — voice coherence, triggering accuracy, boundary correctness, security read for anything foreign-sourced.
3. Eval confirms behavior (skill-creator's eval loop; on claude.ai the description-optimization loop is manual — note this).
4. On approval: stamp truth-level: verified, move the skill folder to /completed-skills, and the work-product copy lands in /corpus.
5. On rejection: back to /forge with specific notes (not "redo it" — name the failure).

## What lives here
Nothing persists — it is a gate, not a store. The skill is in /forge until it passes, then in /completed-skills.

## What good looks like
A deliberate yes or a specific no. The anti-pattern is rubber-stamping — verified should mean a human actually looked. A good rejection names the exact trigger that will misfire or the boundary that is too loose.
